Webhook Reference: order.paid

Emitted by Lumen Commerce when payment is captured. Consumed by Atlas ERP to start fulfillment.

Version
v1 — stable
Direction
Lumen Commerce → Atlas ERP
Delivery
HTTPS POST, JSON body, at-least-once
Last updated
June 2026

1. When it fires

The event fires when the payment provider confirms capture — not on authorization. For orders paid by invoice, it fires when the order passes credit check. It never fires for zero-value orders (full gift-card coverage); those emit order.confirmed only.

2. Payload schema

FieldTypeNotes
event_idstring (UUID)Unique per delivery attempt group; use for idempotency
event_typestringAlways order.paid
sequenceintegerMonotonic per order; discard if lower than last processed
occurred_atstring (RFC 3339)Capture time, UTC
order.idstringLumen order ID; maps to SalesOrder.ExtId
order.total_grossintegerMinor units (cents)
order.currencystringISO 4217
payment.methodstringcard, paypal, invoice, or apple_pay
payment.provider_refstringProvider transaction ID, for refund routing
lines[]arrayOne entry per order line, schema below
lines[].skustringMust exist in the Atlas item master
lines[].qtyintegerUnits; always positive
lines[].unit_price_grossintegerMinor units, after line-level discounts

4. Delivery and retries

Atlas must answer 2xx within 10 seconds. On any other outcome, Lumen retries with exponential backoff: 30s, 2m, 10m, 1h, then hourly up to 24 hours. The event_id stays constant across retries; the delivery header X-Lumen-Attempt carries the attempt number. After 24 hours the event parks in a dead-letter queue and the integration channel is alerted.

Deliveries are at-least-once and ordering is not guaranteed across events. Consumers must treat sequence as the ordering authority per order.

5. Signature verification

Every delivery is signed. The X-Lumen-Signature header carries t=<unix-ts>,v1=<hex> where v1 is HMAC-SHA256 over "<t>.<raw-body>" with the shared endpoint secret. Reject deliveries older than 5 minutes or with a mismatched signature. Secrets rotate via the Atlas integration console; two secrets stay valid during a rotation window.
