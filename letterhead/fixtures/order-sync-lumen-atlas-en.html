Order Sync Integration

How orders stay in sync between Lumen Commerce and Atlas ERP.

1. Context

Lumen Commerce is the storefront where customers place orders. Atlas ERP is the system of record for stock, invoicing, and fulfillment. Today, orders are exported to Atlas in a nightly CSV batch. That delay causes overselling during campaigns and forces support to reconcile mismatched order states by hand.

This integration replaces the batch with an event-driven sync. The goal: an order placed in Lumen is visible in Atlas within seconds, with stock held before the customer sees a confirmation.

2. Scope

In scope: order creation, stock reservation, payment confirmation, order cancellation before shipment.

Out of scope: returns and refunds (covered in the Returns & Refunds Phase 2 document), product catalog sync, customer master data.

3. Flow

The happy path from checkout to a confirmed order:

  1. Customer places the order in Lumen Commerce.
  2. Atlas reserves stock for each line item. Reservation must complete within the 8-second SLA; on timeout, Lumen shows a "confirming your order" state and retries.
  3. Confirmation is sent once stock is held. The order moves to confirmed in both systems.
  4. Payment capture triggers the order.paid webhook from Lumen to Atlas (payload documented separately).
  5. Atlas creates the fulfillment order and returns the Atlas order number, which Lumen stores as external_ref.
Open question: What should the customer see if the Atlas stock reservation times out twice in a row — hold the order in a pending queue, or fail fast and release the cart?

5. Error handling and retries

Reservation timeout

If Atlas does not answer within 8 seconds, Lumen retries once with the same idempotency key. Atlas treats a repeated key as a status query, not a new reservation, so a slow first attempt can never double-hold stock.

Rejected lines

If any line item cannot be reserved, Atlas rejects the whole order with a per-line reason code. Lumen releases the cart and shows which items went out of stock. Partial reservations are deliberately not supported in v1 — split shipments create invoicing edge cases Atlas operations asked to avoid.

Replay and reconciliation

Every event carries a monotonic sequence number per order. A nightly reconciliation job compares order states and flags divergence instead of silently fixing it. Divergence above 0.1% of daily orders pages the on-call.
