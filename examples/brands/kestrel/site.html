What it does

Nightly planning

Imports tomorrow's stops from your fleet system at 22:00 and publishes routes to every cab tablet by 05:00.

Live re-routing

A truck breaks down or a road closes: the remaining stops are spread over the other trucks, and drivers see the change within two minutes.

Constraints that are real

Driver hours, bridge and street weight limits, school-zone time windows, tipping-site opening hours.

An API first

Everything in the planner is available over a documented REST API with webhooks, so your fleet system stays the system of record.
