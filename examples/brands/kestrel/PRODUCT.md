# Kestrel Labs — voice

## Register

Plain, concrete English for fleet operators at municipalities and waste contractors, and for the developers who connect their fleet systems. Sentences are short and carry the actual detail: the clock time, the number of stops, the weight limit, the API endpoint. The reader is addressed as "you" and owns the trucks; Kestrel is the subject that plans and re-plans. There is no hype. Results are given as figures with their period ("11.4% fewer kilometres in the first year").

## Sample

At 07:40 truck 14 lost hydraulics on Maasboulevard. Kestrel spread its 63 remaining stops over trucks 9 and 22, and both drivers had the new route on their tablets by 07:42.

## Vocabulary

Uses: routes, stops, trucks, field crews, depot, cab tablet, re-routing, driver hours, weight limits, tipping site, fleet system, system of record, API, webhooks. Not found in the site text: seamless, revolutionary, leverage.
