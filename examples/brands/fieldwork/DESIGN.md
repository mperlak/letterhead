## 1. Overview

A working studio's paperwork: white page, black ink, one red that means "Fieldwork", and a mustard used sparingly as a fill. Nothing decorative; the content is plans, quotes and status with numbers in them.

## 2. Colors

Red #d9432a is the brand colour for links, the key figure and rules that need emphasis. Mustard #f1c84b fills callouts, tags and highlighted rows, and is never used for text. Body text is near-black #161616 on white.

## 3. Typography

Space Grotesk at 600 for headings, tight and slightly condensed in feel; Source Sans 3 for running text. Big numbers may take Space Grotesk at 700, as the site's figures do.

## 4. Elevation

Flat. The site uses square corners and heavy 2px ink rules instead of shadows, and documents follow that.

## 5. Components

Square-cornered blocks, ink rules between sections, tables over cards. The logo sits on paper at the top of the document in its own colours.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

Do let one figure carry the red. Don't set text in mustard, don't round corners, don't add shadows or gradients.

## 7. Composition

Not measured. Composition comes from the base style until a screenshot, an existing document or the owner's answers say otherwise.
