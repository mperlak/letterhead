# Fieldwork Digital — voice

<!-- Profile drawn from the saved Fieldwork Digital homepage, 2026-10-05. -->

## Register

Fieldwork writes to owners and operations people at companies that make or move physical things. Plain English in the first person plural, short sentences, trade words used as they are (order sync, stock, price lists, freight, ERP). Claims come with a number or a named client; commitments are stated as facts, and bad news is delivered early and without cushioning.

## Sample

Order sync is two days behind plan because the ERP export drops customer price lists over 500 lines. We have a fix in test and expect it live on Thursday. If it slips, you will hear it from us first.

## Vocabulary

Uses: web shop, B2B portal, ERP integration, order sync, discovery, fixed quote, hand over, in writing. Refuses: solutions, seamless, cutting-edge, digital transformation.
