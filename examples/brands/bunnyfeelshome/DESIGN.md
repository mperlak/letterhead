## 1. Overview

BunnyFeelsHome documents go to private clients furnishing a flat or a house. They should look calm and homely, like a well-printed studio proposal, not like a report.

## 2. Colors

The dark green from the logo is both the brand colour and the text colour. The powder pink, from the dot in the logo, fills accents, highlights and rules, never text. The background is the site's warm, slightly creamy page.

## 3. Typography

Headings in Playfair Display at medium weight (500) set the studio's tone. Text in Nunito, a rounded, friendly sans.

## 4. Elevation

No shadows or layers; spacing and thin rules divide the page, as in the base style.

## 5. Components

Components come from the base style, repainted in the brand's colours.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

Use green for text and headings, pink for highlights. Never set text in pink or in pure black.

Composition has not been measured; density, pictures and rhythm come from the base style. A screenshot of the home page or an existing brand document would fill it in.
