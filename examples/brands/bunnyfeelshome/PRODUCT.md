## Reader and register

Private clients furnishing a home who are afraid of costly mistakes. The brand speaks to them as "you", warmly and with confidence, like an experienced interior architect (24 years in the trade, over 3,000 interiors). Sentences are short, sometimes with a vivid contrast ("Between 'I want a nice bedroom' and 'I have a nice bedroom' there is a gap"). Numbers and specifics build trust, not adjectives.

## Sample

You don't have to know everything. We'll prepare a complete design of your interior with a shopping list, so you know what to buy and how it all works together.

## Vocabulary

We use: interior design, ready to build, shopping list, interior architect, one-to-one service, 100% online. We don't use trade jargon without explaining it.
