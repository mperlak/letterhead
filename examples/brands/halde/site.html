How a home comes together

Every project starts with a morning in the house as it is: how the light moves, where the bags get dropped, which chair everyone fights over. We draw only after that. Then one fixed fee for the design, a budget for the build that we keep to, and one person from the studio who answers your calls.

Our approach, step by step

  1. First visit and brief, at your home
  2. Concept: plans, materials, a sample box you can touch
  3. Detailed design and the builder's tender
  4. Site supervision, weekly, until handover
  5. Styling day: we hang, place and light everything
