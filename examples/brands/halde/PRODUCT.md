# Studio Halde: voice

<!-- Read from the Studio Halde homepage, 2026-10-05. -->

## Register

Documents go to private clients, usually households in the middle of a renovation in and around Amsterdam. The studio writes in the first person plural and addresses the client as "you". Sentences are short and concrete: a room, a material, a habit of the people who live there. Commitments are stated outright (one fixed fee, a budget we keep to, one person who answers your calls) without sales warmth around them.

## Sample

The first visit is a morning at your home, before anything is drawn. We will walk the house with you, note how the light moves and where things end up, and leave with a short brief you can correct. The concept follows, with plans, a materials list and a sample box you can hold.

## Vocabulary

Uses: home, rooms, floor plan, materials, sample box, site, builders, handover, styling day, first visit. Refuses: trend talk and showroom language.
