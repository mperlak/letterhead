# PRODUCT.md — Markloop

## What it is

Markloop is a web app for reviewing agent-made HTML documents. Share specs,
reports, and proposals, collect comments and answers in context, then send
the feedback back to your agent.

## Who reads Markloop's documents

- **Builders, developers, consultants** who work in AI coding agents and
  produce documents someone must review or approve. Technical, pragmatic,
  allergic to hype.
- **Their reviewers:** clients, domain experts, teammates. Often
  non-technical; they meet the brand through a shared document link.

## Voice

Concrete, benefit-first, builder-to-builder. Short sentences. Say what the
reader gets, not how the machinery works. No corporate filler, no AI hype
vocabulary, no exclamation marks. Numbers only when they are real.

Example register (from real copy):

- "Your AI agent wrote the doc. Now get human feedback on it."
- "Reviewers see the rendered document, not the source file."
- "Reviewers are always free."

## Vocabulary

- "document", "review", "comment", "answer", "version", "feedback"
- "Viewer" is the product's name for a reviewer role; use it in product
  contexts.
- Avoid: leverage, seamless, revolutionize, supercharge, delve,
  game-changer. Avoid leading with "MCP" or protocol names when writing for
  reviewers.

## Anti-references

- The 2024-era AI-startup landing: purple-cyan gradients, floating 3D
  blobs, "10x your workflow".
- Enterprise-brochure tone: passive voice, capability matrices, buzzword
  bingo.
- Dashboard cosplay in reading documents: fake KPIs, meters, status
  theater.
