# Arkona — voice

## Register

Plain, concrete and craft-minded. Short declarative sentences that name materials, places and numbers. "We" speaks for the workshop; the reader is addressed directly but without sales energy.

## Sample

Larch from the Baltic coast grows slowly, so the wood is dense and resinous. We leave it untreated or oil it once; in two seasons it turns silver-grey and keeps going.

## Vocabulary

Uses: workshop, larch, steel, weathers, repair, trade, project. Avoid filler like solutions, seamless, premium.
