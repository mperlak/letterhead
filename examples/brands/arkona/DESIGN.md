Profile drawn from a saved copy of the Arkona homepage, 2026-10-05.

## 1. Overview

A workshop brand: calm, material, unhurried. Documents read like a well-made letter from a craftsman, not a dashboard.

## 2. Colors

Forest green carries the brand: logo, links, rules, the one accent per page. Sand is the secondary fill for bands, callouts and highlighted rows, never text. Paper is warm off-white, as on the site.

## 3. Typography

Fraunces at 600 for headings and the wordmark; Work Sans for everything you read at length.

## 4. Elevation

Flat. Thin lines and the sand band separate things; no shadows.

## 5. Components

Square-cornered buttons and tags (2px radius on the site). Top rules in green mark the start of a block.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

Do keep one green accent per block. Don't put text on sand in a pale colour, and don't round corners beyond a couple of pixels.

## 7. Composition

Not measured; the base style governs layout until a screenshot or brand book says otherwise.
