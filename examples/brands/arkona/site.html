Outdoor furniture that weathers well

We build benches, tables and loungers from slow-grown Baltic larch and powder-coated steel. No tropical hardwood, no plastic slats, and every piece can be repaired by hand.

See the collections Trade and project enquiries

Built for salt air and long winters

Larch from the Baltic coast grows slowly, so the wood is dense and resinous. We leave it untreated or oil it once; in two seasons it turns silver-grey and keeps going. The steel frames are galvanised before they are coated, which is why our oldest benches on the harbour in Rønne still stand.
