Profile drawn from the Arkona homepage, October 2026.

## 1. Overview
Quiet, workshop-made and practical. Warm off-white paper, one deep green, a soft serif for headings and a plain sans for reading.

## 2. Colors
The green of the logo carries links, rules and emphasis. Warm sand fills callouts and bands. Text is a green-tinted near-black on warm paper, never pure black on white.

## 3. Typography
Fraunces at 600 for headings, Work Sans for body text. Both are open fonts and travel inside each document.

## 4. Elevation
Flat. Thin rules and a top border in the brand green separate blocks; no shadows.

## 5. Components
Square-cornered buttons and tags, small uppercase eyebrows in the green, tables with hairline rows.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts
Do let figures and dates carry the argument. Don't add gradients, glossy surfaces or decorative icons.

## 7. Composition
Not measured; the base style governs density, imagery and rhythm until a screenshot or a real Arkona document says otherwise.
