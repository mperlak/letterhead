# Arkona: voice

## Register
Plain and concrete, with quiet pride in materials and craft. Short declarative sentences, numbers given as facts, the reader addressed directly as a partner rather than sold to.

## Sample
Order sync ran for three days on staging. Of 1,284 test orders, 1,279 reached the ERP; the five that failed share one cause, and the fix is retested on Monday.

## Vocabulary
larch, steel, workshop, trade, collections, repair, weathers, Bornholm.
