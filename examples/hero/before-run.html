Decision needed · Karin & finance

Freeze ERP prices during cutover?

The cutover window runs from 28 October to 3 November. While data moves from the old shop to the new one, any price change made in the ERP has to be handled one of two ways.

Option A · We recommend this

Freeze prices in the ERP

No price changes from 28 Oct to 3 Nov. The cutover stays clean and nobody has to fix anything by hand afterwards.

Option B

No freeze

Prices can change as normal. After go-live we reconcile the changes by hand. We estimate 1–2 days of our time, plus time from your team to check the results.

Please let us know by 10 October.
