Method

How we surveyed the house

On Tuesday 29 September we spent four hours in the house: Liv Halde and Daan Visser from the studio, with Joost van Eck of Van Eck Bouw, the builder we work with most. We went through all three floors, the crawl space and the loft, took moisture readings in the front bay and looked at the fuse box, the wiring and the windows.

The house is a 1932 semi-detached in Kleverpark, Haarlem-Noord, 168 m² over three floors, handed over to you on 1 September. The costs below are Joost's and our rough ranges from what we could see; they are for deciding what goes in the design brief, not quotes.

Finding 1 · Necessary

The wiring needs replacing in full

The fuse box dates from the 1970s and has six groups. Three rooms have no earth, and the wiring in the attic is still cloth-covered.

This is not optional, and it is best done before any plastering, so it sets the order for everything else.

€ 14,000 – 18,000 for a full rewire.

Finding 2 · Necessary

The front bay is damp from the garden side

The lower 60 cm of the bay wall in the living room reads 22–28% moisture. A dry wall here would be under 15%.

The most likely cause is outside the house: the air bricks are blocked, and the garden bed against the facade puts soil above the damp course.

The fix is to clear the air bricks, lower the bed, let the wall dry out over a winter and then re-plaster it with lime: € 4,500 – 7,000.

Finding 3 · Necessary

Two floor joists under the bay have rotted

The ground floor is suspended timber over a crawl space. Two joists at the front are rotten at the ends, where the damp is; the rest are sound. Three of the five crawl-space vents are blocked.

We would replace or splice the two joists, open the vents and insulate under the floor while it is open. The floor needs insulating anyway, so this is the moment: € 6,000 – 9,000.

Finding 4 · Waiting on a test

Two materials may contain asbestos

A flat sheet panel in the boiler cupboard, and the floor tiles under the carpet in the back room, which look like 1960s vinyl tiles.

The test is booked for Wed 7 Oct (€ 450), with results in about ten working days, around Wed 21 Oct. If either is positive, a certified firm removes it: € 3,000 – 8,000, depending on what is found and how much.

Until the results are in

Please leave the boiler cupboard panel and the back-room tiles as they are: no drilling, sanding or lifting.

Finding 5 · Keep

The leaded top lights are worth keeping

The stained-glass top lights (glas-in-lood) at the front are original and in good shape. We would keep them and add inner secondary glazing for warmth and quiet: € 3,800.

The rear windows are 1990s double glazing and are fine as they are.

Finding 6 · Your wish

A kitchen extension fits the garden

The garden is 14 m deep. An extension 3.5 to 4 m deep across the full width of the back is probably permit-free, as a ground-floor rear extension. We will confirm that with the municipality; Joost has built several in Haarlem.

Rough cost including the new kitchen: € 85,000 – 110,000.

Finding 7 · Your wish

The loft can take a study, at a cost

The ridge is 2.10 m high in the middle and the floor is boards over joists. A study up there is possible, but a proper stair would take about 1.2 m² off the smallest bedroom.

Rough cost for the conversion, with the stair, insulation, a dormer at the back and a window: € 28,000 – 36,000.
