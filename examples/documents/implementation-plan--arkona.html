Cutover and go-live plan for the new web shop

We close the old shop on Friday evening 30 Oct, move everything over the weekend and open the new shop on Monday 2 Nov at 08:00. We need Henrik's and Karin's sign-off by Fri 16 Oct to book the weekend.

Summary

Over the weekend of 30 Oct to 2 Nov, we move customer accounts, order history, gift cards and old web addresses into the new shop. Then we switch order and stock sync to the new shop and prove it works with 20 test orders. Two go/no-go calls, on Thu 29 Oct and Sun 1 Nov, decide whether we go ahead. Until Monday 12:00 we can switch back to the old shop. The plan needs three answers from Arkona, listed under Decisions needed. One of them is Henrik's approval of DKK 18,400 for weekend work.

Phases

1. The old shop's content is frozen

Mon 19 Oct · Arkona, with Fieldwork Digital

From this day on, nobody edits products or text in the old shop. Any change goes in the shared sheet, and we re-apply it after go-live. This is also the last day to add package dimensions for the 37 products that are missing them.

Done when: the old shop has no edits after 19 Oct, and every later change is in the shared sheet.

2. The rehearsal shows the migration fits the weekend

Tue 20 – Fri 23 Oct · Tomasz Wójcik

We run a full dress rehearsal on staging with a copy of the live data. The last trial migration took 5 h 40 min, mostly for customers and order history. Tomasz is splitting the order-history import into batches to bring it under 4 hours. On Fri 23 Oct we lower the DNS TTL to 300 seconds, so the Monday switch takes effect within minutes. By the same day, Mette Ravn writes the "shop closed for the weekend" email and phone message, and we review them.

Done when: the rehearsal completes in under 4 hours, the DNS TTL is 300 seconds, and Mette's email and phone message are approved.

Depends on: the copy of the live data, and Mette's texts by Fri 23 Oct.

3. Prices are frozen and the weekend is confirmed

Tue 27 – Thu 29 Oct · Arkona finance, Henrik, Karin, Signe

  1. Tue 27 Oct 06:00
    The ERP price freeze starts.
  2. Thu 29 Oct 14:00
    Go/no-go #1 with Henrik, Karin and Signe, then a walkthrough of the weekend with Jens Kofoed and Mette.

Done when: go/no-go #1 says go, and Jens and Mette know their weekend tasks.

4. The old shop closes and the data moves

Fri 30 Oct 18:00 – Sat 31 Oct ~14:00 · Tomasz, Signe

  1. Fri 18:00
    The old shop shows a maintenance page saying "back Monday 08:00". The last orders are exported and synced.
  2. Sat 08:00
    The final migration starts: customers, order history and gift cards. We start at 06:00 instead if the rehearsal ran over 5 hours.
  3. Sat ~14:00
    Order sync switches to the new shop, and we check the gift-card total.

Done when: all records are in the new shop, order sync runs from the new shop, and the gift-card total is DKK 148,900.

5. Test orders show the new shop works

Sun 1 Nov · Fieldwork Digital, with Arkona's warehouse

We place 20 test orders covering Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Germany, all 4 payment methods and every trade tier. One is a Norway order, to confirm the VAT code fix. Then we refund all of them. Who from Arkona picks the test orders in the warehouse is still open (see Decisions needed).

Done when: every go criterion is met at go/no-go #2, a phone call on Sun 1 Nov at 16:00.

6. The shop opens to customers

Mon 2 Nov · Fieldwork Digital

DNS switches at 07:00, and the shop opens at 08:00. Customers get their "set your new password" email. The price freeze lifts at 12:00, once the first 10 real orders have synced without errors. The full sequence is under Rollout and rollback.

Done when: the shop is open, 10 real orders have synced without errors, and the price freeze is lifted.

7. Hypercare

Mon 2 – Fri 13 Nov · Fieldwork Digital

We're on call on weekdays from 07:00 to 20:00 and on Saturday 7 Nov from 10:00 to 16:00. Each weekday at 09:00 we have a 15-minute check-in with Karin.

Done when: hypercare ends on Fri 13 Nov.

Go criteria

We open the shop only if all six are true at go/no-go #2 on Sun 1 Nov.

  • Under 0.5% of test orders fail to sync. The target is zero.
    Tomasz
  • Stock syncs every 5 minutes or more often. Staging syncs every 15 today. Whether 5 is possible depends on the ERP API rate limits, which Tomasz is checking.
    Tomasz
  • All 4 trade price tiers are loaded, and Sofie has spot-checked 10 customers per tier.
    Sofie Lund
  • Payment webhooks are verified. The provider's sandbox is unreliable, and our support ticket has been open since 29 Sep. If it's still unreliable, we verify with a few small real orders on the live system.
    Tomasz
  • The gift-card total matches DKK 148,900.
    Tomasz, Sofie
  • All of the 300 most-visited old URLs return a 301 redirect to the right page.
    Ines Ferreira

Rollout and rollback

Rollout on Monday 2 Nov

  1. 07:00
    DNS switches to the new shop.
  2. 08:00
    The shop opens, and customers get their "set your new password" email.
  3. 12:00
    The ERP price freeze lifts, once the first 10 real orders have synced without errors.

Rollback

Until Monday 2 Nov at 12:00, we can point DNS back to the old shop, which stays online in read-only mode until 30 Nov. We would key in by hand any orders taken in the new shop before the switch back, and we expect fewer than 30.

After Monday 12:00 there's no switching back. We fix problems in the new shop.
