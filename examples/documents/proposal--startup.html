The situation

Orders reach Atlas ERP once a day. During the spring campaign that delay oversold 412 orders in one weekend, and support now spends about two days a week reconciling order states by hand. Every campaign until the batch is gone repeats this, at campaign scale.

The proposal

Orders visible in Atlas within seconds, stock held before the customer sees a confirmation, and a rollback that is a switch, not a project.

We build event-driven order sync between Lumen Commerce and Atlas ERP: reservation at checkout with an 8-second SLA, payment events driving fulfillment, and the old batch kept as a shadow until the events prove themselves clean. When we are done, overselling ends and the morning reconciliation ritual disappears.

How it would work

  1. Event contract Weeks 1–2. Both teams sign the payload spec; staging credentials in place. Exit: spec committed to both repos.
  2. Reservation flow Weeks 3–4. Stock held at checkout, idempotent on retry, whole-order rejection with readable reason codes. Exit: end-to-end order confirmed in staging.
  3. Payment webhook Weeks 5–6. Signed order.paid events with sequence numbers and a 24-hour retry budget. Exit: duplicates provably create one fulfillment.
  4. Shadow rollout Weeks 7–9. Ramp 5% → 50% → 100% with the batch comparing in shadow; batch retired after 24 clean hours. Exit: zero divergence at every step.

Options considered

  • Run the batch more often (hourly).
    Shrinks the lag, keeps the overselling window and all of the reconciliation work. Cheapest, changes least, fixes least.
  • Replace Atlas ERP outright.
    Solves this and three unrelated years of problems, at a different order of budget and risk. Not this decision.
