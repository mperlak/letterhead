Project recap

Saltværk's new shop is live

Since 1 September 2026, Saltværk has sold from a new Shopify shop in three countries, and every order reaches the warehouse system within a minute. We launched two weeks late. The final invoice was 4.7% over the quote, and all of that is the gift-box configurator you approved in June.

What was delivered

  • A new shop on Shopify with 86 products and storefronts for Denmark, Germany and Sweden, replacing the old hosted shop.
  • Live order and stock sync with the warehouse system (WMS). Every order is in the WMS within one minute.
  • A gift-box configurator where customers choose three salts and a card. This was added by change request.
  • A B2B login for 40 restaurant customers, each with their own prices.

The monthly subscription box was not delivered. Anne moved it to phase 2 in June, because it needs a subscription app and we did not want to rush that choice.

Where the money went

The gift-box change request, approved on Friday 12 June, added DKK 24,000. We finished DKK 4,500 under on the rest of the work, so the final invoice of DKK 431,500 is DKK 19,500 above the original quote.

Why launch was late

On 14 July the WMS vendor changed their order API without notice, which cost us about six working days. We also lost a week to summer holidays we had not planned around. That week is our fault.

Content migration took three times our estimate, because Saltværk rewrote all product texts during the project. The texts are better for it. A weekly written status went out throughout, and Anne read every one.

What we learned

  1. The WMS sandbox does not behave like the vendor's production API. Next time we get a named contact at the vendor in week 1 and test read-only against production early.
    From the 14 July API change
  2. When a client plans to rewrite product texts, content migration is a different job. We will ask about it at kickoff and estimate for it.
    From content migration at 3× estimate
  3. We plan around our own summer holidays before we commit to a launch date.
    From the lost holiday week
  4. The weekly written status is worth keeping. The Thursday calls can be 15 minutes instead of 45.
    From Anne's feedback
  5. A change request that adds a product is worth taking seriously: the gift box became September's best seller.
    From 212 gift boxes in September
