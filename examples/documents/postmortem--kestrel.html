Routes not published by 05:00 on Tue 22 Sep 2026

Summary

The nightly planning for 14 of our 38 customers did not finish. Routes were not on the cab tablets at 05:00, so 211 trucks started without a new plan, and drivers used yesterday's route or paper lists from the depot. The last routes were published at 07:52. Affected trucks started 47 minutes late on average.

The cause was a database change we deployed at night. It locked a table for almost two hours, the planning jobs waited, timed out and did not restart. No data was lost.

We're sorry. Your crews started late because of a change we made, and that's on us.

Why it happened

What set it off

The database change looked safe to run while the system was in use when it was reviewed. But it changed the type of a column, and that rebuilds the whole table and locks it until the rebuild is done.

What turned it into an incident

  • Testing did not show the lock. On our test environment the change took 3 minutes. The test data is about 1/40 of production, so the lock never lasted long enough to matter.
  • Database changes were allowed at night, which is exactly when nightly planning runs.
  • We only alerted on the end result, no plans at 05:00, not on planning jobs that were waiting. We could have known at 02:20.
  • Planning jobs that time out do not restart on their own once the cause goes away. The lock was released at 03:58, but nothing ran again until the manual re-run at 05:20.
