Order Sync Security Review

Webhook and reservation surfaces of the Lumen–Atlas integration, assessed against the integration security checklist, version 2.

Subject
Order sync integration, release candidate 1.4
Criteria
Integration security checklist v2 (January 2026)
Auditor
Security review group, Lumen Commerce
Date
June 17, 2026
Distribution
Lumen engineering, Atlas IT, both team leads

Verdict

Pass, with conditions

The integration meets the checklist with 0 critical, 1 major, and 2 minor findings. The major finding (stale-signature window) must be closed before the public rollout ramp exceeds 50% of orders. The two minor findings may close in the normal release cycle.

A retest of the major finding is required and is triggered by the fix reaching staging.

Scope and criteria

In scope: webhook delivery and signature verification (order.paid), the reservation API including idempotency handling, secret storage and rotation for both surfaces.

Excluded: the Atlas internal network and its operator access model, which are covered by Atlas IT's own annual review, and the payment provider's side of the capture flow, which is outside either team's control.

Criteria: integration security checklist v2. Severity definitions: major means exploitation is practical and impact crosses a trust boundary; minor means defense in depth is weakened without a practical exploit path.

Findings by severity

Stale-signature acceptance window too wide

Major — checklist item 4.2 (replay resistance).

Where
Webhook receiver, verify_signature() timestamp check
Observed
Deliveries with timestamps up to 15 minutes old are accepted; the checklist requires rejection after 5 minutes. A captured delivery can be replayed for 15 minutes.
Why it matters
The replay window multiplies the value of any single intercepted request; 5 minutes is the checklist bar precisely because retries make longer windows unnecessary.
Fix
Tighten the timestamp comparison to 300 seconds. The retry schedule (first retry at 30 seconds) is unaffected.

Reservation errors echo internal field names

Minor — checklist item 6.1 (information exposure).

Where
Reservation API, rejection responses
Observed
Rejected lines return internal column names (itm_mstr.qty_avail) alongside the reason code.
Why it matters
Schema details lower the cost of probing for injection points; the public reason code alone serves the operator.
Fix
Map rejections to the public reason-code set before serialization.

Secret rotation logs at info level in sandbox

Minor — checklist item 5.4 (secrets hygiene).

Where
Sandbox tenants, rotation job logging
Observed
Rotation events log key identifiers (not values) at info level, visible to all sandbox users.
Why it matters
Key identifiers plus rotation timing help an attacker time replay attempts around rotations.
Fix
Move rotation events to the audit log stream; scheduled for 1.4.1.

What passed

  • HMAC-SHA256 signature scheme, including the dual-secret rotation window (item 4.1).
  • Idempotency-key handling under replay: no duplicate stock holds in 48,000 staged orders (item 4.3).
  • Secrets storage: no secrets in code, config, or logs; vault-backed on both sides (items 5.1–5.3).
  • Transport: TLS 1.2 minimum enforced on every surface in scope (item 3.1).
  • Dead-letter queue access limited to the integration group (item 6.3).
