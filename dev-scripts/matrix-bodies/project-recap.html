What was delivered

  • Event-driven order sync live at 100%, batch retired after 24h clean shadow run.
  • Reservation flow with 8s SLA; p95 at 6.1s in production week one.
  • Runbooks: rollback switch, reconciliation, dead-letter handling.

Against the plan

+2 weeks against the nine-week plan; scope itself did not move.

The slip came from staging credentials arriving in week 6 instead of week 1.

How it went

The shadow-run pattern caught one real divergence before customers could. The decision to reject whole orders instead of partial reservations survived contact with production and simplified support macros.

What we learned

  • External credentials belong on the critical path in week zero; next engagement contracts a named owner and an SLA for them.
  • Shadow comparison is cheap insurance; keep it as a permanent nightly guard.
