Checkout Reservation Outage

June 12, 2026 · 41 minutes · severity 2. No orders lost.

Summary

An Atlas item-master job ran outside its window and held table locks during morning traffic. Reservation calls timed out; checkout degraded to the pending state for 41 minutes. The retry design prevented double-holds; no orders were lost.

Impact

1,832 orders saw the pending state; 214 carts were released after double timeouts.

Support received 37 tickets. No stock was double-held and no payment was captured without a confirmed order.

Timeline

  1. 09:14
    Timeout rate crosses 0.5%; on-call paged automatically.
  2. 09:21
    On-call correlates timeouts with item-master lock activity.
  3. 09:38
    Atlas ops terminates the stray job; locks release.
  4. 09:55
    Timeout rate back under 0.1%; incident closed.

Why it happened

Trigger: a manually re-run maintenance job did not carry the window guard the scheduled version has. Condition: nothing in the job runner enforces execution windows; the guard lives only in the schedule definition.

What went well, what went badly

Detection and paging worked as designed (7 minutes to a correct hypothesis). Terminating the job took 17 minutes because the runbook did not cover the manual-run case.
