Why now

The situation

Every campaign oversells stock because orders reach the ERP hours late.

2 days a week of support time go to reconciling order states by hand.

What we do

The proposal

We build event-driven order sync: orders visible in Atlas within seconds, stock held before confirmation, rollback to the batch at the flip of a switch.

Real-time sync is the only option that removes the reconciliation work instead of shrinking it.

How it would work

  1. Weeks 1–2
    Contract. Both teams sign the event payload spec.
  2. Weeks 3–4
    Reservation flow. Stock is held within the 8-second SLA.
  3. Weeks 5–7
    Payment webhook. Confirmed orders reach Atlas once, idempotently.
  4. Weeks 8–9
    Shadow rollout. The batch runs beside the new sync until both agree.

Options considered

  • More frequent batches: lag shrinks but never disappears; reconciliation load stays.
  • Full ERP replacement: solves it and three years of other problems; not this budget.
