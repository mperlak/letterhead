Order Sync Engagement

Proposal: replace the nightly batch with real-time order sync.

Integration team, Lumen Commerce

The situation

Every campaign oversells stock because orders reach the ERP hours late. Support burns two days a week reconciling states by hand.

The proposal

We build event-driven order sync: orders visible in Atlas within seconds, stock held before confirmation, rollback to the batch at the flip of a switch.

How it would work

Four phases over nine weeks: contract, reservation flow, payment webhook, shadow rollout. Each phase has its own acceptance criteria.

Options considered

  • More frequent batches: lag shrinks but never disappears; reconciliation load stays.
  • Full ERP replacement: solves it and three years of other problems; not this budget.
