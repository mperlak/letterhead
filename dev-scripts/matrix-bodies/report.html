Reservation Timeout Analysis

What three weeks of staging traffic say about the 8-second SLA.

Status
Final
Owner

Date
June 2026

Summary of findings

  • Timeouts cluster at Atlas item-master lock windows, not at load peaks.
  • The retry-once policy recovers 92% of timeouts without customer impact.
  • Recommended action: move the nightly item-master job off the campaign window.

What was examined and how

21 days of staging traffic (48k orders), replayed campaign patterns, Atlas lock logs correlated by timestamp.

Finding 1

Timeouts follow lock windows

78% of reservation timeouts fall inside the 02:00–02:20 item-master maintenance window.

Load explains only 9% of variance.

Finding 2

Retry recovers most timeouts

  1. Reschedule the item-master job outside campaign windows.
