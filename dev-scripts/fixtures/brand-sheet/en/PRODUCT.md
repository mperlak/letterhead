# PRODUCT.md — Nimbus Freight (fixture)

Synthetic fixture. Nimbus Freight does not exist — this profile exists only
to exercise brand-sheet.mjs in tests.

## What it is

Nimbus Freight arranges road transport for small and mid-size manufacturers.
A fictional company described for test purposes only.

## Who reads Nimbus Freight's documents

- **Dispatchers and transport planners** (primary): looking for a date, a
  route, a rate.
- **Customers booking transport** (secondary).

## Voice

Plain, short sentences, first person plural: "We arrange transport on the
Gdansk-Munich route." Headings name things, they do not promise outcomes.

Sample register:

- "We arrange road transport for manufacturers across Europe."
- "We confirm a booking within two hours."

## Sample paragraph — how we would write in Nimbus Freight's name

This specification describes how transport bookings are submitted through
the new customer portal. Scope covers the booking form, route confirmation,
and the point where a booking is sent back for correction. Every step is
described the way it should work in the system. Points that cannot yet be
settled are listed separately as questions to Nimbus Freight.

## Vocabulary

- "booking", "route", "dispatcher", "advance notice", "carrier".
- We avoid: marketing superlatives, promises without a date, logistics
  jargon without an explanation.
